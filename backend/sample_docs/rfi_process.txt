Internal Note — RFI Process for New Engineers

An RFI (Request for Information) should be logged in the project tracker
before it is sent to the client or contractor. Every RFI must reference
the relevant spec section using CSI MasterFormat numbering.

New engineers frequently ask who approves RFIs before they go out: RFIs
under $5,000 in potential cost impact may be sent by a project engineer
directly. Anything above that threshold requires project manager sign-off.
