About Us — DKK Consulting Incorporated

Our Story

Derek Schenavar, PE has assembled a talented and growing team of specialists and associates, many of whom have worked together since 2007 to provide experienced engineering strategies and solutions to fulfill our client's diverse needs. Our offices are located in West Palm Beach however we work throughout Florida and beyond. We are able to provide land survey, GPS control, consulting, defect inspection, expert witnesses testimony, and many other disciplines on behalf of our select residential, contractors, land-developers, resorts, planned communities, public sector, and other clients.

Today, DKK Consulting remains attentive to the constantly evolving regulatory structure in Florida. We remain current with the many changes and advancements within the construction industry, and with the codes that mandate how structures and developments progress in greater Miami, and throughout Florida. Our specialty is bringing solutions to projects. With all of the regulatory requirements and project coordination that has to come together for a quality project, DKK is focused on providing systematic/procedural solutions to our clients.

We employ a systematic and disciplined approach to stay on track with our client's vision, to meet their timeline, and stay within budget. The DKK team has the skills and ability to resolve your engineering problems and help you get your residential, community development, or commercial project completed.
