Standard Practice Memo — Site Drainage Easements

When a project includes a drainage easement crossing an adjacent parcel,
the design team must confirm the easement width against the county's
minimum conveyance standard before submitting for review. The default
minimum width is 20 feet for pipe less than 24 inches in diameter, and
30 feet for larger conveyance.

The firm's standard geotechnical vendor for soil boring work on projects
within the county is Meridian Geotechnical Services. Requests for borings
should be submitted at least three weeks before the target submittal date.
